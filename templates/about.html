VIDNETRA

Securing Every Learning Moment

At VidNetra, we craft personalized applications powered by Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence to redefine the way education is experienced. Our solutions give institutions, parents, and students the ability to track attendance, monitor classroom actions, and gain valuable insights into everyday learning.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make every classroom a place where learning is uninterrupted, safe, and deeply engaging. VidNetra harnesses the power of Computer Vision and AI not just to monitor, but to empower — giving educators clarity, parents peace of mind, and students the freedom to focus on growth. By transforming everyday actions into meaningful insights, we aim to preserve the joy of learning while ensuring that every moment in education is secure, connected, and future-ready.

Our Mission

Our mission is to revolutionize the way education is safeguarded and experienced by integrating Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence into everyday learning environments. VidNetra is dedicated to building personalized applications that empower institutions, parents, and students with real-time insights into classroom actions, attendance, and safety.

Core Innovations & Features

Facial Recognition Attendance

High-precision contactless biometric attendance marking with once-per-day logic and automated confirmation emails.

Phone Usage & Distraction AI

YOLOv8 deep learning model detecting real-time spatial overlap between students and mobile devices, with instant faculty alerts.

Crowd & Stampede Prevention

Headcount density monitoring in corridors and halls to detect overcrowding and avert stampede risks before they occur.
